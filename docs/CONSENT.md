# Informed consent (Persian duplex speech study)

You are invited to talk with a research speech system for a BSc thesis at the university. The system may speak while you speak and will try to stop if you interrupt it.

**What is processed live:** your voice is processed by the local speech system during the interaction. No video is required.

**Choose one approved retention mode before the session:**

- Audio: raw voice, derived features, timing/events, ratings, age group, pseudonymous IDs, and optional notes are retained.
- Features: **no raw voice is retained**; only lossy aggregate energy/F0/MFCC features, timing/events, ratings, age group, pseudonymous IDs, and notes are retained.
- Metrics: **no raw voice or acoustic features are retained**; only timing/events, ratings, age group, pseudonymous IDs, and notes are retained.

**Risks:** mild fatigue; the system may misunderstand you or interrupt incorrectly.

**Voluntary:** you may stop at any time. Retained study data can be deleted on request before the thesis is submitted, subject to the university's approved policy.

**Use:** research and the thesis document. Raw audio is never released unless Audio mode was selected and the optional release clause was separately accepted. Existing university data are outside this consent form.
